04 · technique clinic

What to listen for

↕

Pick depth

Expose only enough edge to clear the string. Deep pick travel makes the later crossing studies feel like a wall.

⌁

String crossing

Keep the wrist path continuous. Let the elbow stay quiet while the pick clears one string at a time.

◌

Fret hand

Release pressure immediately after each note. The left hand should feel like a sequence of taps, not a clamp.

↔

Position shifts

For the high-position and long-form sections, move during the last note of a group so the next downstroke lands calmly.

♪

Musical pulse

Accent the first note of each four-note cell, then flatten the accent as the loop becomes automatic.
